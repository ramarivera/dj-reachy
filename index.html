Features

The ultimate robot DJ experience

Powered by ElevenLabs music generation, OpenAI realtime voice, and real-time audio analysis for expressive dancing.

🎹

AI Music Composer

Describe any vibe - "energetic EDM with synth drops" or "chill lo-fi beats" - and DJ Reachy Remix composes an original track on the spot using ElevenLabs.

🎛️

Audio-Reactive Dancing

Real-time FFT analysis detects bass, mids, and treble. The robot's body sways to bass, head bobs to mids, and antennas bounce to treble - all synced to the BPM.

📚

Song Library

Every generated track is saved to your personal library. Play, rename, download, or delete songs anytime from the built-in control panel.

The Flow

From request to performance in seconds

  • 🗣️ Say "Make me a disco track with funky bass"
  • ✅ Reachy confirms your request before generating
  • ⏳ ElevenLabs AI composes your unique song (~1 min)
  • 🔊 Music streams while Reachy dances to the beat
  • 📚 Song saved to your library for replay & download

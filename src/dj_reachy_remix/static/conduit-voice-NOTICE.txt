Conduit voice adaptations in DJ Reachy

subscription_voice.py adapts the consumer context dialect and readiness lifecycle
from GPTLiveProtocol.swift and GPTLiveSession.swift in kaishi00/hermes-conduit,
revision 8abe874ecba738396fc7782288ba6ce1d18c6555.
https://github.com/kaishi00/hermes-conduit/tree/8abe874ecba738396fc7782288ba6ce1d18c6555/Conduit/Voice/GPTLive

voice_media.py and subscription_voice.py adapt PCM pacing, resampling, pending
data-channel delivery and cleanup from dashboard/watch_audio_helper.py.
subscription_broker.py adapts the consumer signaling/session shape from
dashboard/plugin_api.py in hermes-conduit-notifier,
revision 9cf64e1bbccbc57914c5ced8b8b1756df0a6e1a1.
https://github.com/kaishi00/hermes-conduit-notifier/tree/9cf64e1bbccbc57914c5ced8b8b1756df0a6e1a1/dashboard

No Hermes execution code or credential store is required by this fork. The new
broker uses its own explicitly injected Codex credential resolver and fixed app
registry. These notices cover adapted Conduit code only. DJ Reachy's upstream
Apache License 2.0 and history remain unchanged.
